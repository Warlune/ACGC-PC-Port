# Three-town development preview


The opt-in `ACO_DEV_EAST` build (legacy internal option name, defaults OFF) uses a 31-column, 18-row field with 16 outdoor rows. Mainland columns 5 through 25 contain three adjacent towns: a complete newly generated seven-column west town, the original seven-column center town, and a newly generated seven-column east town. Each town retains its mountain borders, native beaches/ocean, railway, river, cliffs, bridges and landmark placements. Southwest and southeast coastal shoulders now connect the towns in the same outdoor scene, around shortened mountain ends. Duplicate stores/stations/landmarks are intentional for this prototype.


`pc/tools/generate_dev_town.c` links the original `m_random_field_ovl.c`, combination table and native RNG at the original 7x10 dimensions. Its small river-direction adapter matches the lookup in `m_random_field.c`. Different saved seeds generate the two sides. Do not run the generator against the widened client dimensions: the game's authored base templates are seven columns wide. The assembly tool copies the corresponding native foreground templates directly from `fgdata.bin` on the user's local disc; these are real generated layouts, not mirrored copies or hand-painted grass.


`tools/aco/make-world-preview.py` assembles the generated sides around a copy of a normal GAFE01 save, then applies the shared `world-generation.py` coastal rule. It validates source/output checksums, preserves all 30 original playable foreground acres and buried-item flags byte-for-byte at a twelve-acre eastward offset, copies the fixed fields around resized arrays, and mirrors the main save into its backup sector. Only the shared border columns receive mountain caps, connecting beaches and ocean. It refuses to overwrite an output or edit the source. Current OCE2 expanded saves are 614464 bytes, **not standard GameCube saves**. Layout JSON comes from compiling `pc/tools/dev_east_layout.c` with and without the development define. Keep this runtime/save directory isolated from normal builds.


`Build World Dev.cmd` builds the client. Launch with `--dev-world`, optionally `--dev-west` or `--dev-east`. The dev boot chooses an existing resident, enables the RTC normally initialized by the title, skips character/title setup, and chooses an empty tile on a flat acre in the selected region. Every restart reuses that resident. Resetti is disabled and continuous acre movement enabled in the dev copy.


`--dev-sw` and `--dev-se` start on the coastal connections. Add `--dev-coast-test` to walk each route to the adjacent town and back through normal controller/collision code; it prints PASS only after the return. This test mode is never enabled by the regular player launchers. `connect-world-coast.py` upgrades a copy of an existing triple-town development save using the same shared rule; it also refuses overwrites. `test-world-generation.py GENERATOR DISC LAYOUT --seeds 60` verifies generated layouts, interior preservation, clear shoulders and idempotence. Route metadata supplies future story-gate coordinates; gates are not enabled yet.


Internal ridges now use modular straight faces, rounded corners, front sections and grass plateau pieces (`pc_dev_ridge.inc`). The 240-unit cliff height, two rock tiers, ledges and texture density follow the native cliff profile. Mesh coordinates are world units; the only renderer scale is the ordinary 1/16 asset conversion. Each half ridge is 640 units wide; the end has a 160-unit corner radius and stops at world Z=3760, leaving a dry shoulder before the beach. The shared generator rule installs flat, empty base acres for these pieces, and the client installs flat cliff collision cells and native diagonal wall cells for the rounded ends during field initialization. The old stretched models and resampled coastal-cap collision are removed. Exterior world borders keep the original assets. This remains prototype terrain; the collision grid has the game's 40-unit resolution. Its two-cell corner chamfer stays within 24 units of the rounded visual foot; unlike a continuous height ramp, the native diagonal cells create actual walls.


The automated coast test additionally walks around both corners, pushes into each cliff and retreats before reporting PASS. It rejects entering a different scene or climbing above the walking surface. Test windows are hidden so they do not disturb a separate interactive session. Run southwest and southeast separately against isolated development saves. These are targeted regression routes, not proof that every generated town edge is free of obstructions.


The PC renderer now retains 16 foreground acres and draws a 5x5 terrain region; slot bitfields support all 16 entries. Retained slots survive window changes, outgoing slots are freed before replacements, and interior scenes retain their original allocation counts. Actor culling and outdoor far clipping expand with camera distance above 100%. Texture-cache lookups use indexed buckets with full content/palette identity and rebuild after eviction. Diagnostic reports are buffered and flushed once per report to avoid synchronous writes per printf field, especially on NAS-backed paths.


This is a terrain/layout preview. Global town services, NPCs, landmark uniqueness, original map-panel labels, schedules/train service and world metadata still assume one logical town. Duplicated buildings are not independent fully integrated businesses. Story locks and co-op integration remain further work. The faint minimap now renders actual terrain meshes from directly overhead across all three towns; foreground actors are not included in that pass. See `minimap.md`.



The ocean extends five acres beyond each side of the 21-acre mainland while retaining its existing depth. Exterior mountain walls occupy columns 0 and 30 and continue to the southern rim at row 15. Native coastal endcaps and rotated native cliff pieces connect the northern ocean ledges to the mainland. The island is the actual saved two-acre island at (23,12)-(24,12); only the east mainland port at (24,6) remains. West/center dock terrain and boat/sign anchors are removed. The original eight-point boat route keeps its berthing curves, moves to the east port and lengthens the open-water leg. A complete interactive ferry journey still needs separate validation.

The last two rows (16 and 17) store authored indoor templates outside the outdoor map. Deleted island copies are removed from those rows too, so native global island searches see only one island. Planting and buried-item arrays remain six rows deep. Event/NPC placement stays on the mainland, and sea acres have no planting/deposit pointers. NPC reservation initialization uses integer counters to handle the larger field safely.

The shared generator rule applies this layout to newly assembled worlds. Existing development saves migrate into separate copies: `expand-world-ocean.py` converts the legacy 21x10 world to OCE1, then `widen-world-ocean.py` converts OCE1 to OCE2. Both require source and destination layout JSON from `pc/tools/dev_east_layout.c`, preserve the source, reject overwrites, validate checksums and mirror the backup. Widening preserves all 114 old planting/deposit acres at a five-acre X offset, relocates resident/bridge coordinates and keeps the saved island contents. The client refuses older development layouts before interpreting or writing them. Normal build/save dimensions remain unchanged.

The inaccessible plateau behind each exterior wall uses original-density 160-unit pieces and two acres of camera overscan. No decorative mountain-top trees are added. Optional `--dev-outer-west` / `--dev-outer-east` boots inspect mainland outer corners. With `--dev-coast-test`, these starts run a field audit for unique port/island, safe planting/deposit pointers and southern rim collision.

Validation: normal and OCE2 builds plus minimap layout/OpenGL tests pass; shared generation passes 60 native seeds; the migrated OCE2 world loads and passes its field audit. The full-map export renders every outdoor acre at one scale, including all water and the saved island. These checks do not establish manual boating, independent regional services or co-op readiness.
